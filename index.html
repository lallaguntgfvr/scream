✳ 01 / the person behind the pixels

a small introduction

hello, from my side of the screen.

I'm Mori online. A quiet collector of tiny things: old game cases, pretty screenshots, song lyrics that feel like letters, and bits of the internet that still feel handmade.

I love stories that leave a little ache behind — dreamy anime, odd little games, visual novels, and music for staring out the window. When I'm not wandering through fictional worlds, I'm usually making something, saving references, or rearranging this page for the hundredth time.

This site is a scrapbook more than a profile. It's where I can be earnest about the things I love, leave small notes for future-me, and make a corner of the web feel like mine. Thanks for visiting; I hope you find something that makes you smile.
